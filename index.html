Performance Trends
  • Launch day 18 Nov carried the most volume (138,817 impressions) but the weakest CTR at 0.16%.

Creative Analysis
  • 320×480 (vertical mobile) is the standout at 8.42% CTR — 879 clicks from just 10,438 impressions. Alongside 300×600 (3.61%), the two formats delivered 47.5% of all clicks from 6.4% of impressions.
  • All four vertical sizes combined returned a 5.41% CTR on 24,068 impressions, against 0.42% for the horizontal banners (284,648 impressions).
  • 320×50 was the workhorse: 187,309 impressions (54.7% of delivery) at a 0.47% CTR, just under benchmark, and the largest single source of clicks after 320×480 (877).

Device Analysis
  • Smartphone drove the campaign: 325,308 impressions (95.0%) and 2,677 clicks (98.4%) at a 0.82% CTR, in line with attendees using their phones on-site.
  • Tablet delivered a small volume (3,609 impressions) but a healthy 0.69% CTR, also above benchmark.
  • Desktop saw minimal delivery (13,547 impressions) and the weakest engagement at 0.13% CTR.

Publisher Intelligence
  • News is the strongest category at scale: 2.80% CTR and 983 clicks (36.1% of the total) from just 35,165 impressions (10.3%).
  • Entertainment & lifestyle carried the most volume — 153,630 impressions (44.9%) — and the most clicks (1,243, 45.7%) at a 0.81% CTR.
  • Books & reading posted the highest CTR at 11.07%, but on only 551 impressions (0.2% of delivery). Email, calls & phone tools took 122,176 impressions (35.7%) at a lower 0.28% CTR.
